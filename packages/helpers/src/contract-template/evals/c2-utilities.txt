TENANCY AGREEMENT (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)
Between Palm Test Estates and the Tenant.

Article 5 - Utilities. The Tenant shall keep the DEWA and cooling accounts in the Tenant's own name and pay all bills when due.
Article 6 - Termination. Either party who wishes to end the tenancy early must give the other party at least 90 days written notice.
Article 7 - Payment. Rent is payable by post-dated cheques. A bounced cheque will be treated as a breach of contract.
Article 8 - Condition. The Tenant shall keep the Unit clean and return it in the condition received, fair wear and tear excepted.
