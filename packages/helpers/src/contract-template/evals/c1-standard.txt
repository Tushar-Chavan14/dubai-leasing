RESIDENTIAL TENANCY CONTRACT (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)
Landlord: Example Properties LLC. Tenant: A. Sample.

1. Maintenance. The Tenant shall report any defect to the Landlord in writing and shall pay for minor repairs costing less than AED 500 per incident.
2. Pets. No pets are allowed in the Unit without the Landlord's prior written consent.
3. Subletting. The Tenant may not sublet or share the Unit with any person not named in this contract.
4. Parking. One covered parking bay, number 14, is included with the Unit.
